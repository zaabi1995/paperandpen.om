Ali Adnan Haider Darwish is the Omani founder and CEO of BHD Group, the Muscat group that Paper & Pen is part of. He founded the group in 2019. He is also Head of Strategic Planning at ITHCA Group, Oman's sovereign technology fund. This profile sets out who he is and how his work connects to the software on this site.

## From a print shop to 14 companies

BHD Group takes its name from Ali's grandfather. BHD stands for Bin Haider Darwish LLC. The group started with one business, BHD Printing & Designing in Muscat. Today it has 14 companies in printing, technology, software platforms and sovereign AI.

Paper & Pen is one of those companies. Its sister companies include:

- Hosn, sovereign on-premises AI
- Mithaq, board governance software
- Cardify, digital business cards
- Dardasha, a WhatsApp business platform
- ReachScreens, Q+, arabian.ceo and Splitty

Ali is also a co-founder of The Flower Bar.

## What Paper & Pen does

Paper & Pen is a cloud ERP for businesses worldwide. Paper and Pen Company LLC makes it in Muscat, Oman. Sales and invoicing are free forever. A business pays only when it adds modules such as inventory, HR, accounting or manufacturing.

The platform is Arabic-first. It works in any currency, and each business sets its own tax rate. The [features](/features/) page lists every module, and the [pricing](/pricing/) page shows what each one costs.

## A career in Oman's economy

Ali started his career in 2017. His early roles were at the Central Bank of Oman, EY, Oman Aviation Group and Oman Broadband.

From November 2021 to June 2023 he was Head of Business Environment at the Oman Vision 2040 Office. In that role he built the Invest in Oman centre. He also worked on the National Investment Strategy, which narrowed 43 sectors to 8 priority sectors.

He joined ITHCA Group as Head of Strategic Planning in February 2025. Since October 2023 he has been Secretary to the Board of DataOm, the submarine cable company of ITHCA Group. He has also been a board member of the Oman Fencing Committee since 2023.

## Study and recognition

Ali holds two degrees from Brunel University London: a BSc in Economics and Management, and an MSc in Sustainability, Entrepreneurship and Design. He is a doctoral candidate in Innovation and Management at the University of Central Lancashire. Before university, he earned the IB Diploma at The Sultan's School in Oman.

In November 2025 the Family Business Council Gulf gave him its Next Generation Award. He was the first Omani to win it. The [FBCG awards record](https://web.archive.org/web/20260116060731/https://www.fbcgawards.com/) lists him among the 2025 winners.
